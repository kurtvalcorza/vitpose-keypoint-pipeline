## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) estimated the synthetic drawing from its drawn box and reported `sample-sanity` PCK against the drawn joints. This notebook keeps that drawing and its two-stage inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real labelled photograph corpus fetched with per-file pins and degraded under a stated recipe, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (PCK, mean OKS, per category), two box-only baselines, a bounded fine-tuning with the heatmap MSE, a per-category breakdown on the small persons and their clean counterparts, the drawing and four held-out persons re-estimated after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make the shared weights root working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries both 4-file snapshot manifests inline and asserts each against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` and `stage_missing_detector_files(DETECTOR_WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetch only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revisions (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` and `verify_detector_snapshot` re-hash every entry before either processor or model is constructed; `VitPoseKeypointPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR, detector_dir=DETECTOR_WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or either model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is 300 COCO val2017 photographs whose Flickr licence is CC BY 2.0 or CC BY-SA 2.0 (COCO licence ids 4 and 5), each pinned in the carried `samples.py` by byte size and SHA-256 and fetched from `http://images.cocodataset.org/val2017/<id>.jpg` (about 52 MB); a cached or fetched file is refused on any mismatch, and every record keeps its Flickr page and licence. Their 498 persons (non-crowd, at least 12 labelled joints, box at least 120 px tall) carry the 2017 keypoint annotations (CC BY 4.0) inline. `read_corpus` makes each person a record: `degrade` downscales the photograph so the box is 40 px tall and re-encodes it as JPEG 30 with the box and joints scaled; `category` = labelling density (`full` / `partial` / `sparse`); `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 180 / 45 / 75 photographs (persons follow their photograph) and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no photograph (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared; `read_corpus(..., target_height=None)` builds the clean counterparts of the test persons. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the byte count and the licences.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `detect_people` + `estimate` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the synthetic drawing (inference contract) → `box_centre_baseline` + `mean_pose_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model over the small persons and the clean counterparts with the per-category breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation, test and the clean counterparts with the comparison (evaluation) → `estimate` + `evaluation_report` on the drawing again and the skeleton panels (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `VitPoseKeypointPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** every keypoint `score` is the joint's heatmap maximum after the affine warp, not a calibrated probability; the corpus PCK and OKS are measured against COCO's labels at stated tolerances (PCK at `PCK_FRACTION` of the box's longest side, OKS with COCO's per-joint sigmas and the box area); no acceptance threshold ships. The drawing re-estimated after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the box-centre and mean-pose baselines, the frozen model and the clean counterparts are scored on the same 136 persons as the adapted model, on both measures and per category; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample under one degradation with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly that the frozen model sits near the mean-pose prior on small persons and that the gain is measured under this degradation only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and photograph-disjoint; validation (72 persons) selects the epoch on the mean of PCK and OKS and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that photographs from few cameras or sessions must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `MAX_PERSONS` (50), both thresholds, `PCK_FRACTION` (0.1), `MIN_LABELLED` (12), the record bounds (8..5,000) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the models run; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each row of `keypoints.csv` becomes a labelled person as given (no degradation), and the records flow through the same validation, photograph-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected layout and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/vitpose_keypoint_train.csv`.
- Licence: both checkpoints are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; the photographs carry their Flickr licences (CC BY 2.0 / CC BY-SA 2.0), the keypoint annotations are CC BY 4.0, and neither is redistributed — the export records the corpus name, release, host, byte count, licences and degradation under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `vitpose_keypoint_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, category, size, box, labelled joints, scale, provenance)
- `vitpose_keypoint_input_manifest.json` — the inference-contract manifest (schema, per-input observations, one recorded rejection finding)
- `vitpose_keypoint_scene_frozen.png`, `vitpose_keypoint_scene_adapted.png` — the drawing with its estimated skeleton before and after adaptation
- `vitpose_keypoint_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics on validation, test and the clean counterparts, the per-category comparison, the adaptation history
- `vitpose_keypoint_examples/` — four held-out small persons as side-by-side panels (frozen skeleton, adapted skeleton, labelled joints; 4× zoom)
- `vitpose_keypoint_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `vitpose_keypoint_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, both model identities, snapshot, corpus and degradation, inference-contract reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime

The earlier records for the superseded `TASK-INFERENCE` carrier are kept in `../docs/release-verification.md`.
